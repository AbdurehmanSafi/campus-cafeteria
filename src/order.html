How to Order

Your favourite food is just four simple steps away!

Place Your Order in 4 Easy Steps

01

Choose Your Items

Explore our menu and choose your favourite meals, snacks and beverages.

02

Pay at the Counter

Confirm your selected items and pay at the cafeteria counter.

03

Collect Your Token

Keep your order token safe. You will need it to identify your order.

04

Pick Up Your Food

When your token number is called, collect your food from the pickup counter.

Important Guidelines

  • Avoid peak lunch hours if you want to save time.
  • Keep your token until you receive your order.
  • Check your items before leaving the counter.
  • Do not crowd the pickup counter.
  • Keep the cafeteria clean and use the bins.
